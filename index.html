Help centre

Frequently Asked Questions

How early should I arrive at the airport?

For international flights we recommend arriving at least three hours before departure, and two hours for domestic flights.

How much baggage can I carry?

Baggage allowance depends on your route and fare type. Check the details shown at booking or in your e-ticket.

Can I change or cancel my booking?

Yes. Use “Manage Booking” with your booking reference and last name. Fees and conditions depend on your fare.

How do I earn ShebaMiles?

Add your ShebaMiles number when booking and miles are credited after you fly.
